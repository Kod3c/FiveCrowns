📖 Five Crowns Rules

🎯 Objective

Be the player with the lowest score after 11 rounds!

🎮 Gameplay

  • 11 Rounds: Each round uses more cards (Round 1 = 3 cards, Round 11 = 13 cards)
  • Wild Cards: Each round has a different wild card (3s through Kings)
  • Your Turn: Draw one card (from deck or discard pile), then discard one card

🃏 Valid Groups

  • Sets: 3 or more cards of the same rank (e.g., 7♥ 7♠ 7♦)
  • Runs: 3 or more consecutive cards in the same suit (e.g., 5♥ 6♥ 7♥)
  • Wild cards can substitute for any card

👑 Going Out

When all your cards form valid sets or runs, click "Go Out" to end the round. Other players get one more turn.

📊 Scoring

  • 3-9: Face value (3 = 3 points, 9 = 9 points)
  • 10: 10 points
  • Jack: 11 points
  • Queen: 12 points
  • King: 13 points
  • Joker (Wild): 50 points
